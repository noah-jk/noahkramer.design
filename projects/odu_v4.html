[ ACT 01 — 2021 ]

Bulletin Board Syndrome

Announcements. News feeds. Events. No recruitment content. We rebuilt it around one question: what does a prospective student need to see first?

→ Traffic to the right pages followed.

[ ACT 02 — 2022 ]

One University, Two Identities

ODU acquired Eastern Virginia Medical School. EVMS wanted their own subdomain and identity. ODU wanted conformance. We designed the middle ground — expanded components for everyone, a pre-footer unique to EVMS pages.

→ The political problem was the design problem.
[ ACT 03 — 2025 ]

Learn From Anywhere

Online students ask three things: What can I study? What will it cost? How do I apply? The old ODU Global site made all three hard. We organized everything around those questions.

→ Shorter paths to key actions. Better findability. Happier authors.
