[ SOLUTION 01 ]

Get Them in the Door

Mission College's student journey was like a 3 legged stool: programs, free tuition and steps to apply. We created numerous entry points to these and made sure they all included links to each other.

A prominent "Start Here" link above the fold on the homepage offers students clear direction and leads to a page teaching them how to be a student (key for our first-gen audience).

→ Higher Ed decisions aren't linear. Give clear entries and keep them moving.
[ SOLUTION 02 ]

Build a digital welcome center

We worked closely with the admissions team to make the web process clear and proactively offer hints. Since most of our students are local, we always are reminding them of the walk-in help center

→ Be helpful and convert quickly.
[ SOLUTION 03 ]

Too Much Stuff

The previous site had such long dropdowns they went off the page! Every node was included in the navigation.

  1. 01 Reduced overall navigation items. Moved to a hub and spoke model for larger sections.
  2. 02 Audited pages and reduced old and outdated content by 60%.
  3. 03 I added a tapbar on mobile so the most important links are always with you.
[ PROCESS ]

Solo Run

Personnel changed mid-project. I ran research, synthesis, and stakeholder work solo. AI made that feasible in a way it wouldn't have been 18 months earlier.

→ Deliverables: focus groups, surveys, UX testing, wireframes, visual designs & prototypes
