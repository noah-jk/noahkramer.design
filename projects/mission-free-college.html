[ SOLUTION 01 ]

Get Them in the Door

Mission College's student journey was like a 3 legged stool: programs, free tuition and steps to apply. We created numerous entry points to these and made sure they all included links to each other.

A prominent "Start Here" link above the fold on the homeage offers students clear direction and leads to a page teaching them how to be a student (key for our first-gen audience).

→ Higher Ed decisions aren't lienar. Give clear entries and keep them moving.
